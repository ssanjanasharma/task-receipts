HELP & FAQ

How it works:

1. Add a task to your list (only one active task is allowed at a time).

2. Select the task and click Play to start focusing.

3. Pause when you need a break.

4. Click Complete to sign off your session (via webcam snapshot or hand-drawn signature) and print your receipt.

5. Open "Session summary" to view, drag, and stack all your focus receipts on the metallic tray.

FAQ:

Q: Why can I only have one active task?

A: To maintain focus! True focus means concentrating on one thing at a time.

Q: How do I clear my session?

A: Click "End session" on the summary screen. It will clear all tasks and history.
